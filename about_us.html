About Furnished By Persi



Furnished By Persi is a South African luxury furniture business founded and owned by Palesa Madibela. Palesa started the company at the age of 31 with a passion for interior design, quality furniture, and creating beautiful living spaces.

The business was established with the aim of providing customers with stylish, durable, and high-quality furniture without making luxury completely unaffordable.Furnished By Persi focuses on combining modern designs with comfort and functionality.

Furnished By Persi currently operates through branches in Cape Town, Johannesburg, and KwaZulu-Natal. Each branch is committed to providing customers with professional service and a wide selection of furniture for homes, apartments, offices, and other spaces.


Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality luxury furniture that transforms ordinary spaces into beautiful, comfortable, and stylish environments while maintaining excellent customer service and competitive pricing.


Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of South Africa's leading luxury furniture brands by providing exceptional furniture, innovative designs, and a memorable shopping experience to customers across the country.
