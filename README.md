## PROJECT OVERVIEW
# WEBSITE GOALS AND OBJECTIVES
To offer the necessary information about available care services that is easily accessible and attract more volunteers and financial sponsors through the collection of leads.

# KPI
Reduction in phone enquiries for basic information already available on the site (pricing, hours, location)

## WEBSITE ANALYSIS
# SPECIFIC GOALS
•	Showcase the full Furnished By Persi product range (lounge suites, dining sets, bedroom furniture, coffee tables, and custom furniture) with clear pricing
•	Generate qualified leads through an online furniture enquiry form
•	Provide branch location, contact, and business hours information for all three branches
•	Build brand credibility and communicate the company's luxury positioning to a South African audience
•	Give visitors enough information to make a purchase decision or enquiry without needing to call first

# Strengths & Weaknesses
STRENGTH's:
All three branches and the contacts number are listed which makes it easier for the target market to locate our business. 
Our customer's are able to navigate through our price ranges. 
Our broad product range, that include lounge suites, bedroom furniture, coffee tables, including custom and delivery services covers most of a customer's home in one place.

WEAKNESSES:
A visitor can't get to my lead-generation form without knowning the exact URL, which is a direct hit to conversions.
There is no other visible social proof such as reviews, that luxury furniture shoppers often look for before buying.

# Improvement Areas
•	Connect the enquiry form to a working backend or third-party form handle
Introduce responsive styling so the site displays correctly on mobile devices

# Proposed Features and Functionality
The website will be designed using five pages, including the Homepage, About Us, Services, Enquiry, and Contact page. The Homepage will contain general information about the business, while the About Us page will focus on the business's mission. The Services page will provide the list of services to be offered by the business. The Enquiry page will contain a form for customers to use when contacting the business . The Contact page will have the location map of the business's offices.

## Design and User Experience
# Layout
A single-column, image-led layout with a consistent header (logo, tagline, navigation) and footer (copyright, email, project name) across every page, so visitors always know which site they are on and how to get back to the homepage.
