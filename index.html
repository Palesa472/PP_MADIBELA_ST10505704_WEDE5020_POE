At Furnished By Persi, we provide high-quality and stylish furniture designed to bring comfort, elegance, and personality into every home. Our furniture is carefully selected to meet the needs of clients who value quality, modern design, and luxury.

We offer a wide range of luxury furniture, including lounge suites, dining sets, bedroom furniture, coffee tables, and custom furniture solutions. Our goal is to provide beautiful furniture at competitive prices while delivering excellent customer service. Ready to buy? Send us an enquiry.

Furnished By Persi was founded by Palesa Madibela, who started the company at the age of 20 with a vision of creating a furniture brand that combines affordability, quality, and luxury. Today, the business serves customers through branches in Cape Town, Johannesburg, and KwaZulu-Natal. Read our story.
